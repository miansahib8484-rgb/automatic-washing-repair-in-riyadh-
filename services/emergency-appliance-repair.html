What Counts as an Appliance Emergency?ما الذي يعتبر حالة طارئة؟

Water leaking onto the floor, a burning smell, sparking, an appliance that won't switch off, or a fridge/freezer that's stopped cooling and risks spoiling food — these are treated as emergencies. We prioritize these calls and aim to reach you the same day, any day of the week.
