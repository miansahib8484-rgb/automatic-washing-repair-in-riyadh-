What Does Fridge Repair Involve?ما الذي يشمله تصليح الثلاجة؟

Fridge repair means checking the cooling system — compressor, thermostat, door seal, fan and drain line — to find why the refrigerator isn't cooling properly, then repairing the fault so the fridge holds a safe, consistent temperature again.
