Common LG Washing Machine Error Codesرموز أعطال غسالات LG الشائعة

CodeWhat It Usually Means
OEDrain error — water isn't draining out, often a blocked filter, pump or hose.
UEUnbalanced load detected during spin — sometimes fixed by redistributing laundry, otherwise a suspension or sensor fault.
LEMotor error or locked rotor — usually needs a technician to inspect the motor and wiring.
dE / DEDoor error — the door isn't detected as closed or locked properly.
PEWater level sensor (pressure switch) fault.
tETemperature sensor (thermistor) error.
IEWater inlet error — the machine isn't filling with water as expected.

Error codes can vary slightly by LG model year. If the code keeps returning after a reset, it's worth having a technician diagnose it rather than repeating the cycle.
