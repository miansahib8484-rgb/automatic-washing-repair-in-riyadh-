Repair or Replace Your Washing Machine?تصليح أم استبدال الغسالة؟

Quick Answer

Should You Repair or Replace a Broken Washing Machine?هل تصلح الغسالة أم تستبدلها؟

As a rule of thumb: if the machine is under 8 years old and the repair cost is less than about half the price of a comparable new machine, repairing is usually the better value. Past that age, or after several repeat repairs, replacing often makes more sense.

كقاعدة عامة: إذا كان عمر الغسالة أقل من 8 سنوات وتكلفة الإصلاح أقل من نصف سعر غسالة جديدة مماثلة، فالإصلاح عادة هو الخيار الأفضل. بعد هذا العمر، أو بعد عدة إصلاحات متكررة، الاستبدال غالباً يكون أكثر منطقية.

Nobody wants to spend money fixing an appliance that's about to fail again anyway — but replacing a machine that only needed a simple part swap is money down the drain too. Here's how to think it through.

1. Check the Machine's Age

Most automatic washing machines are built to last roughly 8 to 12 years with normal use. Under 5 years old, almost any repair is worth doing. Past 10 years, it's worth weighing the fault against the machine's remaining lifespan.

2. Compare the Repair Cost to a New Machine

A widely used guideline is the "50% rule": if the repair cost is more than about half the price of a new, comparable machine, replacement usually offers better long-term value — especially on an older unit.

3. Look at the Repair History

One repair on an otherwise reliable machine is normal. Multiple unrelated faults within a year or two is a sign the machine is wearing out overall, even if each individual repair is affordable.

4. Consider the Type of Fault

Some faults are simple and cheap regardless of the machine's age — a door lock, drain pump, belt or water inlet valve, for example. Others, like a failed motor or control board on an old machine, are a stronger signal to consider replacing.

5. Factor in Water and Energy Efficiency

Washing machines from over a decade ago typically use noticeably more water and electricity per cycle than current models. On an older machine, that ongoing cost is worth weighing alongside the repair bill itself.
