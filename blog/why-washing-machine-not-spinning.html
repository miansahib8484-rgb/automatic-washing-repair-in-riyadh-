Why Is My Washing Machine Not Spinning?لماذا لا تدور الغسالة؟

September 10, 2026By Fanni Automatic TeamWashing Machine Repair

A washing machine that fills and washes but won't spin is one of the most common calls we get. Here are the usual causes, starting with the simplest.

1. Overloaded or Unbalanced Load

Too many clothes, or a single heavy item like a duvet, can throw the drum off balance. Most machines have a safety sensor that stops spinning if the load isn't balanced. Try redistributing the load or washing heavy items separately.

2. Worn Drive Belt

On some machines, a rubber belt connects the motor to the drum. Over time this belt can wear, stretch or snap, so the drum fills and washes but doesn't spin. This is a straightforward part to replace.

3. Faulty Door Lock

Most automatic washing machines won't spin unless the door lock sensor confirms the door is properly closed and locked. A faulty lock mechanism can prevent spinning even when the door looks closed.

4. Drain Pump Blockage

If the machine can't drain the water properly, it usually won't proceed to the spin cycle. A blocked drain pump filter or hose is a common and often simple fix.

5. Motor or Control Board Fault

If none of the above apply, the issue may be with the motor itself or the electronic control board. This is best diagnosed by a technician with the right testing equipment.

When to Call a Technician

If redistributing the load and checking the door don't help, or if you hear a burning smell or unusual noise during the spin attempt, it's time to book a professional diagnosis rather than keep running the machine.
