Refrigerator warning signs

A fridge rarely fails all at once — it usually gives a few warnings first. Catching these early can save your groceries and avoid a bigger repair bill.

1. Food Spoiling Faster Than Usual

If milk turns or vegetables wilt sooner than expected, the fridge may not be holding a consistent temperature, even if it still feels cold to the touch near the door.

2. Warm Spots Inside the Fridge

Uneven cooling — cold at the top, warm at the back, or warm spots near the door — often points to a fan, vent blockage or thermostat issue.

3. Compressor Running Constantly or Very Loudly

Some compressor hum is normal, but a compressor that runs non-stop, cycles very frequently, or has become noticeably louder is working harder than it should.

4. Excess Frost or Condensation

Heavy frost build-up in the freezer section, or condensation collecting inside the fridge compartment, can indicate a door seal problem or a failing defrost system.

5. Water Pooling Under or Inside the Fridge

A blocked or frozen drain line often causes water to collect inside the fridge or leak out underneath — usually straightforward to clear once diagnosed.

6. Noticeably Higher Electricity Bills

A refrigerator working harder to maintain temperature — due to a worn seal, failing compressor or blocked coils — draws more power, which often shows up in your bill before you notice a change in cooling.

When to Call a Technician

If you notice two or more of these signs together, or anything that puts stored food at risk, it's worth booking a diagnosis rather than waiting for a full breakdown.
